Candopia — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026 (version 2 — includes advertising)
Developer: Naresh Parmar (Candopia)
Contact: jaymogaltravels45@gmail.com

The game itself

Candopia does not collect, store or transmit any personal data of its own. Your game progress — unlocked levels, stars, coins, lives and settings — is saved only in a local file on your device, never leaves your device, and is deleted when you uninstall the game or clear the app's data. The game contains no accounts, no analytics of our own, and no real-money purchases. Items in the in-game shop are bought only with coins earned by playing.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

Candopia shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google and its ad partners may collect and process data such as: your device's advertising ID, device information (model, operating system, language), IP address (used to estimate general location), and information about your interactions with ads.

Candopia is configured for child-directed, family-safe advertising: ads are non-personalised (not based on your interests or browsing), content is limited to a "G" (general audiences) rating, and Google is instructed to treat every ad request as child-directed. You can learn how Google uses data from apps that use its services here: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Where required by law (for example in the EEA/UK), you will be shown a consent message before ads load, and you can change your choice later from the game's Settings.

Children

Candopia is suitable for general audiences including children. Beyond the non-personalised, G-rated advertising described above, no information about children or any other player is collected by the developer.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Candopia changes how data is handled, this policy will be updated before that version is released and the changes will be disclosed in the store listing.
